Finding the Perfect Family Pool in UAE

After five years of exploring resorts across Dubai and Abu Dhabi with my family, I've discovered pools that truly stand out. These aren't just places to swim—they're destinations where memories are made.

From infinity pools overlooking the Arabian Gulf to water parks that keep kids entertained for hours, here are my personal recommendations based on real experiences.

Source: Unsplash
Luxury Resort

Atlantis The Palm: Where Adventure Meets Relaxation

The moment we stepped into Atlantis The Palm, my kids' eyes lit up. This isn't just a pool—it's an entire aquatic playground. The resort features multiple pools, but the family pool area with its shallow sections and nearby Aquaventure waterpark access makes it unbeatable for families.

What impressed me most was the attention to safety. Lifeguards are positioned strategically, and the pool areas are designed with families in mind. The temperature-controlled pools mean comfortable swimming year-round, even during Dubai's cooler months.

All-Inclusive

Rixos The Palm: Your Family's Private Oasis

If you're looking for an all-inclusive experience where you don't have to think about anything, Rixos The Palm delivers. We spent a week here last summer, and the pool setup is designed specifically for families who want everything in one place.

The separate children's pool area is a game-changer. While my husband and I relaxed by the main pool with its stunning beach views, our kids had their own space with age-appropriate activities. The poolside service is exceptional—fresh towels, drinks, and snacks appear before you even ask.

Sky-High Experience

Address Beach Resort: Swimming Above the Clouds

This one is special. The Address Beach Resort boasts the world's highest infinity pool, and swimming there feels like floating above Dubai. At 77 stories high, the views are absolutely breathtaking—you can see the entire coastline and the iconic Burj Al Arab in the distance.

While it's more sophisticated than a typical family pool, the resort has created family-friendly hours and areas. We visited during their family time slots, and the staff went above and beyond to ensure our children felt comfortable. The pool temperature is perfect, and the surrounding deck area provides plenty of space for families to spread out.

Why These Recommendations Matter

I'm not a travel blogger or influencer. I'm a mother of two who moved to Dubai in 2019 and has been exploring family-friendly destinations ever since. Every recommendation here comes from actual visits, real experiences, and honest observations about what works for families.

These pools aren't just beautiful—they're practical. They have proper safety measures, family amenities, and staff who understand that traveling with children requires different considerations. Whether you're planning a weekend staycation or a longer vacation, these spots have proven themselves time and again.
