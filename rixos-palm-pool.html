Rixos The Palm: Your Family's Private Oasis

March 15, 2025 • Last Updated: March 2025

Source: Unsplash

After hearing about Rixos The Palm's all-inclusive approach, I was intrigued. As a parent, the idea of not worrying about every meal, drink, or activity sounded appealing. We booked a week-long stay in August 2024, and it turned out to be one of our most relaxing family vacations.

The All-Inclusive Pool Experience

What sets Rixos apart is how the pool experience integrates with the all-inclusive concept. You don't just get pool access—you get poolside service that's included in your stay. Fresh towels appear before you need them, drinks are brought to your lounger, and snacks are available throughout the day without additional charges.

The main pool is beautifully designed with a view that stretches to the beach and beyond. It's large enough that even when the resort is at capacity, it doesn't feel crowded. The depth varies, with shallow areas perfect for families with younger children and deeper sections for swimming.

Source: Unsplash

The Children's Pool Area

This is where Rixos truly shines for families. The separate children's pool area is thoughtfully designed. It's not just a smaller version of the adult pool—it's a dedicated space with features that keep children engaged. The water temperature is slightly warmer, which my kids appreciated, and the depth is consistent and safe.

What impressed me most was the supervision. While parents are always responsible for their children, the staff in the children's area are trained to interact with kids appropriately. They organize simple activities, ensure safety, and create an environment where children feel comfortable.

Beach Access Integration

The resort's location on Palm Jumeirah means direct beach access, and the pool area flows seamlessly to the beach. This dual experience—pool and beach—means you're not choosing between options. You can spend the morning at the pool, have lunch, then move to the beach for the afternoon.

During our stay, we developed a routine: pool time in the morning when the sun was less intense, beach time in the afternoon, then back to the pool for evening swimming. The proximity makes this transition effortless, even with children in tow.

Poolside Service Excellence

The all-inclusive service extends to the pool area in ways that matter. Fresh fruit is brought around regularly, ice cream is available for children (and adults who want it), and the drink service is prompt without being intrusive. The staff remembers your preferences, which adds a personal touch that makes the experience feel more like a private club than a resort.

Source: Unsplash

Practical Family Benefits

No Hidden Costs

One of the biggest advantages is the lack of surprise charges. Everything at the pool is included—towels, drinks, snacks, even pool toys for children. This makes budgeting straightforward and eliminates the stress of constantly checking prices.

Flexibility

The all-inclusive model means you can try different things without worrying about cost. Want to order a smoothie? It's included. Need extra towels? No problem. This freedom enhances the relaxation factor significantly.

What to Know Before You Go

Rixos is an all-inclusive resort, which means the pricing structure is different from pay-as-you-go options. The initial cost might seem higher, but when you factor in meals, drinks, and activities, it often works out better for families, especially those with children who eat frequently.

The resort attracts families, which creates a welcoming atmosphere. Children make friends easily, and parents can relax knowing their kids are in a safe, family-oriented environment.

Best Times to Visit

We visited in August, which is hot but manageable with the pool access. The pool areas have good shade coverage, and the water temperature is comfortable year-round. For cooler weather, the pools are heated, making any time of year suitable for swimming.

Final Recommendation

Rixos The Palm is ideal for families who want a worry-free vacation. The pool facilities are excellent, the all-inclusive approach eliminates decision fatigue, and the family-friendly atmosphere makes it easy to relax. If you're looking for a place where you can truly disconnect and let the resort handle everything, this is it.
