About Me

Hello, I'm Sarah Mitchell. I'm not a travel blogger, influencer, or professional reviewer. I'm a mother of two who moved to Dubai in 2019 when my husband's job brought us here. What started as a temporary assignment has become our home, and along the way, I've discovered something important: finding the right places for family activities isn't always straightforward.

Why This Site Exists

When we first arrived in Dubai, I spent hours researching family-friendly activities online. I found plenty of glossy marketing photos and promotional content, but very little honest, practical information from parents who had actually been there with their children. I wanted to know about safety, practical considerations, and real experiences—not just beautiful pictures.

After five years of exploring resorts, hotels, and pool facilities across Dubai and Abu Dhabi with my family, I've accumulated enough experience to share. This site is my way of providing the kind of information I wish I'd had when we first arrived.

My Approach

Every recommendation on this site comes from actual visits. I don't accept sponsored stays or promotional partnerships. When I write about a pool or resort, it's because we've been there, experienced it as a family, and I believe it's worth sharing.

I focus on practical details that matter to families: safety measures, child-friendly features, accessibility, and honest assessments of what works and what doesn't. I also consider value—not just price, but what you get for your investment.

What You Can Expect

Honest, practical information based on real experiences. I'll tell you if a pool area gets too crowded, if the safety measures are adequate, if the family amenities are genuinely useful, and whether the experience matches the marketing.

I'll also share practical tips: best times to visit, what to bring, what to expect, and things to consider before you go. These details can make the difference between a great family day and a stressful one.

My Family

I have two children—a seven-year-old and a ten-year-old. Our experiences are shaped by their ages and needs, which means I can speak to what works for families with school-age children. As they grow, our experiences evolve, and I'll continue updating recommendations based on our changing needs.

Beyond the Pools

While this site focuses on pools, I'm always exploring new family-friendly destinations across the UAE. Dubai and Abu Dhabi offer incredible opportunities for families, and I believe in sharing information that helps other parents make the most of their time here.

If you have questions, suggestions, or want to share your own experiences, I'd love to hear from you. You can reach me through the contact page.

Thank You

Thank you for visiting this site. I hope the information here helps you find pools and experiences that create wonderful memories for your family. That's ultimately what this is about—helping families discover places where they can relax, have fun, and enjoy time together.

Happy swimming!

Sarah Mitchell
Dubai, United Arab Emirates
