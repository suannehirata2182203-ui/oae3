Address Beach Resort: Swimming Above the Clouds

March 15, 2025 • Last Updated: March 2025

Source: Unsplash

When I first heard about Address Beach Resort's infinity pool being the highest in the world, I was skeptical. How different could it really be? After visiting in December 2024, I can confirm: it's genuinely extraordinary. At 77 stories high, swimming there feels like floating above Dubai itself.

The Sky-High Experience

The pool itself is stunning—a long infinity pool that seems to merge with the sky. But what makes it special isn't just the height; it's the views. From the pool deck, you can see the entire Dubai coastline, the iconic Burj Al Arab, and on clear days, the distant mountains. It's a perspective of Dubai that most people never experience.

The water temperature is perfectly maintained. Even in December, when we visited, the pool was warm enough for comfortable swimming. The pool area includes both shallow and deeper sections, though it's designed more for adults and older children than toddlers.

Source: Unsplash

Family-Friendly Considerations

I'll be honest: this isn't a traditional family pool. It's more sophisticated, more adult-oriented. However, the resort has made thoughtful accommodations for families. They have designated family time slots when the atmosphere is more welcoming to children, and the staff is trained to help families feel comfortable.

My children, aged 7 and 10, were initially intimidated by the height and the sophisticated atmosphere. But once we explained the experience and they got in the water, they loved it. The novelty of swimming so high up was exciting for them, and they still talk about it months later.

Safety at Height

Safety is paramount, and the pool area reflects this. The barriers are substantial and well-designed, providing security without obstructing views. Lifeguards are present and attentive, and the pool deck has non-slip surfaces throughout. For families with younger children, constant supervision is essential, but the safety measures provide additional peace of mind.

The Pool Deck Experience

The deck area around the pool is spacious and well-appointed. There's plenty of room to spread out, which is important for families. The loungers are comfortable, and the service is attentive without being intrusive. The poolside restaurant offers excellent options, though it's more upscale than typical family pool dining.

Source: Unsplash

Best Times for Families

The resort offers family-friendly hours, typically in the mornings and early afternoons. During these times, the atmosphere is more relaxed, and families with children are more common. Evening hours tend to be more adult-oriented, with a sophisticated, social atmosphere.

We visited during the family hours, and it was perfect. The pool wasn't crowded, the staff was particularly attentive to our children, and we felt completely comfortable. I'd recommend calling ahead to confirm current family hours, as they can vary by season.

What Makes It Worth It

This isn't just a pool—it's an experience. The combination of the height, the views, and the quality of the facilities creates something memorable. For families with older children or teenagers, it's particularly special. The "wow factor" is real, and it's something your family will remember.

The resort itself is luxurious, and the pool experience reflects that. If you're looking for a unique, sophisticated swimming experience that offers something different from typical resort pools, this is it.

Practical Tips

  • Book in advance, especially for family time slots
  • Bring cameras—the photo opportunities are incredible
  • Consider the time of day—morning light provides the best views
  • Dress appropriately—the atmosphere is more upscale than casual pool areas
  • Be prepared for the height if anyone in your family is sensitive to heights

Final Thoughts

Address Beach Resort's pool is an experience, not just a place to swim. It's sophisticated, unique, and offers something you won't find elsewhere. While it's not a traditional family pool, the resort has made it accessible to families who want this unique experience.

If you're looking for something special, something that will create lasting memories, this is worth considering. It's not the most family-oriented pool on this list, but it's certainly the most unique.

For reservations and current family hours, visit the Address Hotels website or check availability through Expedia.
