Atlantis The Palm: Where Adventure Meets Relaxation

March 15, 2025 • Last Updated: March 2025

Source: Unsplash

When we first visited Atlantis The Palm in 2020, I wasn't sure what to expect. The resort's reputation precedes it, but as a parent, I needed to know if it lived up to the hype for families. Three visits later, I can confidently say it does—with some important caveats.

The Pool Experience

Atlantis doesn't have just one pool—it has multiple pool areas, each serving different purposes. The main family pool area is where we spent most of our time. It's expansive, with sections that gradually deepen, making it perfect for children of different ages. My seven-year-old could stand comfortably in the shallow end while my ten-year-old practiced swimming in deeper sections.

Source: Unsplash

What Makes It Special

The temperature control is excellent. Even in January, when Dubai's weather can be cooler, the pools are heated to a comfortable temperature. This means year-round swimming, which is crucial for families planning visits during different seasons.

The lifeguard presence is professional and reassuring. During our visits, I noticed lifeguards actively monitoring the pools, not just sitting in chairs. They engage with children, remind them of safety rules, and maintain a watchful presence that made me feel secure letting my kids swim.

Aquaventure Waterpark Connection

What sets Atlantis apart is the direct access to Aquaventure Waterpark. As a resort guest, you get unlimited access, and the proximity means you can easily move between the pool areas and the waterpark throughout the day. This flexibility is invaluable with children who have varying energy levels.

During our last visit, we'd start at the pool in the morning, head to Aquaventure for the afternoon when the kids wanted more excitement, then return to the pool for a calmer evening swim. This kind of flexibility isn't available at most resorts.

Practical Considerations

Best Times to Visit

Weekday mornings are quieter. If you want a more relaxed pool experience, aim for weekdays. Weekends, especially during school holidays, can get quite busy. The pool areas are large enough to accommodate crowds, but the atmosphere changes significantly.

What to Bring

  • Sun protection is essential—the pool areas have some shade, but not everywhere
  • Water shoes for children—the pool decks can get hot
  • Towels are provided, but bringing your own can be helpful during peak times
  • Snacks and water—while poolside service is available, having your own supplies saves time and money

Family Amenities

The resort understands families. There are changing facilities near the pools, storage lockers available, and the poolside restaurant has a children's menu that goes beyond typical kids' food. My children particularly enjoyed the fresh fruit options and smoothies available throughout the day.

Source: Unsplash

Things to Consider

Atlantis is a premium resort, and the pricing reflects that. If you're looking for budget-friendly options, this might not be the best fit. However, if you're planning a special family vacation and want everything in one place, the convenience and quality justify the cost.

The resort can feel large and busy. If you prefer intimate, quiet pool experiences, Atlantis might feel overwhelming. But if your family enjoys energy and activity, it's perfect.

Final Thoughts

Atlantis The Palm's pool facilities are among the best we've experienced in the UAE. The combination of quality facilities, safety measures, and access to Aquaventure makes it a standout choice for families. It's not just about swimming—it's about creating a complete aquatic experience that keeps everyone entertained.

For more information about Atlantis The Palm, visit their official website or check out reviews on TripAdvisor.
